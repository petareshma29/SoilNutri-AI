SOIL NUTRIENT GUIDE

Know what your soil needs.

Understand the role of NPK, pH, organic matter and moisture before making fertilizer or crop decisions.

N

Nitrogen

Supports leafy growth and plant development. The required amount varies by crop and soil.

P

Phosphorus

Important for roots, flowering and energy transfer.

K

Potassium

Supports water regulation, plant strength and stress tolerance.

⚗️

pH

Indicates soil acidity or alkalinity and influences nutrient availability.

🌿

Organic Matter

Helps soil structure, water holding capacity and biological activity.

💧

Moisture

Helps determine irrigation needs and supports healthy root activity.
